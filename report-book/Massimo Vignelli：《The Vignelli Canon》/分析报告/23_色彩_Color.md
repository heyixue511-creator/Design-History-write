# 23 章节分析报告：色彩（Color）

## 一、章节定位与功能

本章将色彩定位为"能指"（Signifier）和"识别符"（Identifier）——不是装饰性元素，而是**信息传达的功能性工具**。这与 Typefaces 章的"结构重于类型"有着完全一致的逻辑：有限的调色板，清晰的信息传达。

## 三、内容分析

### L1 核心论题

**大多数时候我们将色彩用作能指或识别符——一般不以图像方式使用色彩。我们倾向于偏好原色调色板（红、蓝、黄）。在企业识别系统中，色彩型（Chromotype）与标识型（Logotype）共同构成识别符。适切性是选择色彩的规则——在正确的时间使用正确的色彩。**

### L2 关键论点

| 论点 | 原文依据 |
|------|---------|
| 色彩用作能指（Signifier）或识别符（Identifier）——非图像性使用 | "Most of the time we use color as a Signifier, or as an Identifier. Generally speaking we do not use color in a pictorial manner." |
| 偏好原色调色板——红、蓝、黄 | "we tend to prefer a primary palette of Red, Blue, and Yellow." |
| 在企业识别中，色彩型与标识型等共同构成识别符 | "Chromotype becomes the Identifier along with the Logotype or other devices (morphotypes, phonotypes etc.)" |
| 也使用全光谱表达情绪、感受、激情、内涵 | "We have used the entire spectrum of colors to express moods, feelings, passions, connotations and more." |
| 色彩使用方式：如字体——有限且清晰的调色板 | "as we do with typefaces, we have limited and articulated our palette to express the message in the clearest and most understandable way." |
| 有时需要强烈的原色，有时需要微妙的粉彩，有时仅黑白，有时深棕色和暖色调 | "There are times for strong primary colors and times for subtle pastel colors..." |
| "适切性是我们在选择色彩时使用的规则之一" | "Appropriateness is one of the rules we use in choosing colors" |

## 七、语言文风

> **L7.1** "Most of the time we use color as a Signifier, or as an Identifier. Generally speaking we do not use color in a pictorial manner."

> **L7.2** "Appropriateness is one of the rules we use in choosing colors knowing how effective it can be to use the right color at the right time."
