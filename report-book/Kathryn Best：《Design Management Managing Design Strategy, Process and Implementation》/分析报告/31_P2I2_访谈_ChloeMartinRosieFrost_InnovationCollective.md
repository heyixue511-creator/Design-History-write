# 31_P2I2：访谈——Chloe Martin & Rosie Frost（The Innovation Collective）

## 一、章节定位与功能
本访谈展示了"小型创新网络"（associate model）这一替代性组织模式。Chloe Martin（创始人）和Rosie Frost（副合伙人）代表了后?What If!时代的独立创新实践者网络——无固定办公室、无层级、按项目组建的团队。

## 二至七节（概要）

### L2 核心论题
1. **"Associate Model"**：基于项目需求组建团队——"We build a project team tailored to each job"——无固定层级/无办公室（使用私人会员俱乐部）/最小管理费用
2. **"客户买的是人，不是机构"**："Increasingly, clients are buying people – not agencies – so allowing smaller, more interesting and diverse start-ups like us to emerge"
3. **规划是创造力的使能者**："Detailed process planning actually allows you to be more creative...This 'plan' is underpinned by an aligned agreement of where you need to get to and when. This gives you permission to play with the how you get there!"
4. **"每个人都是创造性的"**："Simple behavior training and guidance on how to land a good idea is enough to dispel this myth"

### L2 关键论点
- L3 "People ARE your biggest asset in an organization – they are your brand and they create your culture."
- L3 "Collaboration gets you to newer and better ideas faster. It also allows you to act big even if you're small."
- L3 "With madness there is always measure."
