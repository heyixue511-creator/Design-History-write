# 47_P3I2：访谈——Colette Liebenberg（Colette Liebenberg Design）

## 一、章节定位与功能

### L2 定位
本访谈代表"独立设计顾问"视角——一位在南非出生、伦敦成长的室内设计师，从Kajima→DTZ→Unilever全球设计经理→2014年在英国乡村创办个人设计咨询公司。她的职业轨迹跨越了设计管理的所有关键角色：设计师→高级设计师→部门主管→全球设计经理→独立创业者。

### L2 功能
- **职业全生命周期叙事功能**：从手绘笔+墨水的绘图板→AutoCAD→全球设计指南→Agile Workplace策略——展示了一个设计师/设计经理的30年技能进化
- **Agile Workplace概念展示功能**：Unilever的"Agile"定义——"to allow people to work anywhere, any place, so long as the business needs are met"——工作场所设计从"为个体"转向"为活动"
- **独立创业操作功能**：将家中一个房间改造为办公室/主墙涂成黑板→"visually seeing this map everyday keeps me inspired"

## 二至八节（概要）

### L2 核心论题
1. **Agile Workplace的原则**：工作场所围绕活动（activity）而非个体（individual）设计；可持续性是日常待办清单上最重要的项目之一；技术驱动最终决策（BIM/视频会议/远程协作）
2. **"管理是做正确的事；领导是做正确的事"**："'Management is doing things right; leadership is doing the right things.' Peter Drucker."
3. **"设计管理在无意识中就滑入了创业过程"**："It is truly amazing how design management slips into the set up process without even realizing it."
4. **远程全球客户管理**：在乡村生活/通过在线会议呈现和沟通/全球设计指南和工具包可以远程创建——"Companies now want to discourage unnecessary travel, so they empower local staff to get involved on local projects."

### L2 关键论点
- L3 "Agile is a working standard in its own right...'to allow people to work anywhere, any place, so long as the business needs are met'."
- L3 "'Management is doing things right; leadership is doing the right things.' Peter Drucker."
- L3 "Having skills such as design, project planning, communication and presentation skills, diplomacy, empathy, and the ability to understand people and their behaviors, are all key for the job."
