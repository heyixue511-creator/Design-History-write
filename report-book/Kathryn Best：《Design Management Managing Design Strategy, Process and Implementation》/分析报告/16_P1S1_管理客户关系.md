# 16_P1S1：关键技能——管理客户关系（Managing Client Relations）

### L2 定位
本章是Part One KEY SKILLS层次的第一章，标志着从"知识理解"（KNOWLEDGE）和"情境体验"（PRACTICE）向"个人能力建设"（KEY SKILLS）的过渡。它直接对应Part One KNOWLEDGE中"建立设计战略"和"推广销售设计战略"中涉及的客户关系维度。

## 三至七节（概要）

### L2 核心论题
1. **以客户为中心的方法**：从提问而非假设开始——"ask questions directly...develop an insight into the client's perspective"
2. **关系作为商业资产**：客户不仅期望设计团队交付质量和利润，也期望"可靠的工作关系"
3. **透明度作为信任基础**：客户可能不理解预算是如何被使用的→"design or project managers need to keep their client informed"
4. **会议管理的结构化**：会前（议程、参与者筛选、文件分发）→会中（逐项处理、主席管理跑题、先易后难建立节奏）→会后（会议记录撰写与分发）

### L2 原文摘录
- L3 "'If we want to know what a business is, we have to start with its purpose. And the purpose must lie outside the business itself...There is only one valid definition of a business purpose: to create a customer.' Peter Drucker."
- L3 "Clients may not know what to expect from an internal design team or external design consultancy, in which case, the design or project manager needs to keep the client informed and up-to-date."
- L3 "'Clients want chemistry, understanding, loyalty, commitment, trust, respect, integrity, passion, collaboration and partnership.' Ralph Ardill."

### L3 文风特征
1. **操作性祈使句密集**："nominating...""holding...""agreeing...""writing...""creating...""defining..."——六个动名词并列的指导语链
2. **客户期望列举**：Ralph Ardill的十个期望名词（chemistry/understanding/loyalty/commitment/trust/respect/integrity/passion/collaboration/partnership）——构成客户期望的"十诫"
