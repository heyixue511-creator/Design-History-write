# 32_P2S1：关键技能——管理创意团队（Managing Creative Teams）

## 一、章节定位与功能
本章是Part Two KEY SKILLS的首章，将设计管理的焦点从"流程"转向"人"。核心论题是三个词：**freedom, focus and budget**（自由、聚焦和预算）——这是设计经理管理创意团队的三个关键杠杆。

## 二至七节（概要）

### L2 核心论题
1. **自由-聚焦-预算三角**："The freedom to allow time and space for divergent thinking, the focus to then encourage convergent thinking, and a realistic awareness of the budget"
2. **"化学"高于"结构"**："the informal conversations that occur, and the actual 'chemistry' of the team, which will stimulate the sort of creative results"
3. **结构-指导-领导力三维管理**：结构（同意角色/责任/任务→评审时机→决策流程→目标/截止日期/里程碑）；指导（保持团队知情→共享信息→反馈→建设性批评→表扬）；领导力（团队需要有人"in charge"→冲突管理→移除障碍）
4. **微观管理的危害**："Stifling creativity by micromanaging, controlling and dominating...are all detrimental to the nature of the creative process."

### L2 关键论点
- L3 "The key words for anyone responsible for managing a creative team are freedom, focus and budget."
- L3 "Learning to be comfortable with a certain level of imprecision is part of being a design manager."
- L3 "Design managers often have to exercise judgement and compromise between honoring the client agreement and trusting the team's creative ability."
