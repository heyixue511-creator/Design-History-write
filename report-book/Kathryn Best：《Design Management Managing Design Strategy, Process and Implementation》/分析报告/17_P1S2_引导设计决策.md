# 17_P1S2：关键技能——引导设计决策（Guiding Design Decisions）

## 一、章节定位与功能

### L2 定位
本章延续"管理客户关系"的讨论，将焦点从"关系维护"转向"决策引导"——即设计/项目经理如何在与客户的互动中积极塑造设计决策的方向和质量。

### L2 功能
- **信任建设功能**：通过透明度（预算使用说明）、保密协议（NDA）和管理期望来减少客户对"未知的恐惧"
- **教育功能**：将设计经理定位为"教育者"——"Good design or project managers educate clients in the ways that design can help business compete"
- **倡导功能**：鼓励设计经理培养"设计积极型客户"（design-positive clients）

## 二至七节（概要）

### L2 核心论题
1. **消除决策障碍**："barriers in the client relationship and decision-making process need to be identified and removed"
2. **预算透明度与信任**：客户的常见担忧是不理解预算如何被使用——"This concern can raise questions over the level of trust...and soon develop into fear of the unknown"
3. **"教育即服务"**：花时间辅导客户关于设计知识、解释项目结构和团队组成——设计经理是客户的设计素养教育者
4. **学会说"不"**："the need to be both assertive and empathetic, and to be able to say 'no' if required"——引导决策不仅是"说服"，也包括"拒绝"
5. **将客户培养为设计赞助人**："Inspiring clients...to take pride in their role as potential design patrons and champions"——客户从被动接受者到主动倡导者的转化

### L2 原文摘录
- L3 "A common client concern is a lack of understanding about how and where their budget is being spent. This concern can raise questions over the level of trust...and soon develop into fear of the unknown."
- L3 "Good design or project managers educate clients in the ways that design can help business compete and add value to their products or services."
- L3 "Translating unfamiliar terms or progressing conversations by, for example, explaining the economic benefits of good design...puts design into the language spoken by the client."

### L3 文风特征
1. **情感诊断语言**："fear of the unknown"——将客户对预算的担忧诊断为一种情感反应（恐惧）而非理性判断
2. **"翻译"隐喻**："translating unfamiliar terms""puts design into the language spoken by the client"——将设计经理的工作定义为跨语言翻译
3. **"教育+倡导"双动词**："educate""mentor""guide""inspire"——四个递进动词丰富了设计经理的角色内涵
