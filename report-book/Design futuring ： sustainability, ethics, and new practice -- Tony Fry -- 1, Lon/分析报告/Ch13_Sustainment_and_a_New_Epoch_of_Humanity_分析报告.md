# 第13章 分析报告：Sustainment and a New Epoch of Humanity

---

## 一、章节定位与功能

本章是Part III的**文明哲学章**，将Sustainment提升至与启蒙运动（the Enlightenment）相提并论的历史高度。Fry在此完成了全书的"宏大叙事"——从12000年前定居生活的起源，到当代"unsettlement"时代的来临，将redirective practice置于人类文明史的长时段语境中。

---

## 二、核心论题

**论题一：Sustainment作为"新启蒙"**

"the Sustainment as an opening moment and process is posed against functionalist and ever more linguistically evacuated uses of the concept of sustainability"（L1454）。Fry明确将Sustainment与启蒙运动类比——"the Enlightenment existed as a promoted idea prior to becoming a generalized cultural condition... The Sustainment is offered as a prefigurative idea demanding realization through design"（L1462-1454）。

**论题二：从游牧到定居再到"未定居"（unsettlement）**

（L1434-1440）：12000年前的定居革命→当代气候变化驱动的新一轮"unsettlement"——"Having moved from non-settlement (the nomadic life) to settlement, we may well be heading toward an unknown condition of unsettlement"（L1440）。

**论题三：Sustainment的辩证法**

（L1466-1481）：Fry对"the dialectic of sustainment"进行了全书最系统的哲学阐述——追溯了辩证法的历史（Socrates→Plato→Zeno→Aristotle→Abelard→Kant→Hegel→Marx→Adorno），最终定义为："destruction and creation are indivisibly implicated in each other – the one always coexisting with the other"（L1478）。

**论题四：再制造（Remaking）**

（L1482-1490）：介于消除与创造之间的"remaking"——不仅是物质性的改造（retrofitting），更是"the intellectual project of exposing the foundations of thought to remake thinking"（L1486）。援引Confucius的"exemplary person"（chün tzu, L1487）作为转向性实践者的理想形象。
