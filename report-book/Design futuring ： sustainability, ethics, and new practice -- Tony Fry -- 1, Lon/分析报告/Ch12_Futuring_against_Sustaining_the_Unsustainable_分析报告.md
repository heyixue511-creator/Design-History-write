# 第12章 分析报告：Futuring against Sustaining the Unsustainable

---

## 一、章节定位与功能

本章是Part III "Design, Sustainment and Futures"的**批判性开场章**，对现有五大可持续设计/发展立场进行系统性审视与批判。Fry在此展示了其"经由否定达至肯定"的论证策略——通过对技术拯救主义、可持续设计、产品服务化、可持续消费、人道主义设计的一一解构，为后续章节的Sustainment建构清理地基。

## 三、核心论题

**论题一：技术拯救主义的幻觉**

"the most basic, obvious and important action just does not get a look-in – this is the need to eliminate the need for so much energy!"（L1359）。NASA夜间地球图像——越亮越富裕也越不可持续——作为技术幻象的视觉隐喻。

**论题二：对"三重底线"的虚无化批判**

"the crude stool or the more elegant triangular fractal tile of McDonough and Braungart are representational illusions with no actual referent"（L1351）。Fry对McDonough/Braungart的cradle-to-cradle模型也持保留态度——"it can even obstruct perceptions of relational connections"（L1383）。

**论题三：绿色建筑的关键不在建筑本身**

"people can make a 'green' building unsustainable and likewise an unsustainable building can contribute to sustainment by the way, and for what, it is used"（L1374）。这是对LEED等评级体系根本性缺陷的诊断。

**论题四：可持续消费话语的理论贫困**

消费作为经济范畴（consumption）与作为生态范畴（metabolism）不可通约——"all durable products at the end of useful life have not been consumed"（L1399）。援引Heidegger的"thinging of things"和François Jullien的"propensity of things"以凸显现有话语的理论不足（L1407）。

**论题五：人道主义设计的政治幼稚**

Architecture for Humanity被批判为"feel-good, apolitical politics"（L1413）——缺乏将灾难置于unsustainability框架中的能力。英国Disaster Institute在1970s已发现：提供预制避难所往往破坏社区的应对机制。
