# 第9章 分析报告：Methods of Change 2 – Designing in Time

---

## 一、章节定位与功能

本章是Part II方法论三部曲的**时间维度篇**，提出全书最具方法论原创性的概念——**Designing in Time**（时间中的设计）——作为redirective practice的核心方法。Fry将时间从"度量"（measure）重新定义为"媒介"（medium），追溯至Aristotle对时间的定义："the event in which things occur"（L1059），并将设计实践从空间主导转向时间主导：从过去学习、面向未来设计。

## 三、核心论题

**论题一：未来不是虚空**

"the future is filled with the attainments and mistakes of the past"（L1063）。因为温室气体有200年以上的大气寿命，我们已生活在"过去"投掷到"未来"的后果中。

**论题二：Futuring Scenario Building**

场景构建不是预测，而是探索"what potentialities beg interrogation"（L1071）。基本方法：从未来时间点反向设计——"design back from that moment"（L1075）。

**论题三：Design Scenarios vs. Scenarios of Design**

（L1099-1127）：Fry批判了Ezio Manzini和François Jégou的Sustainable Everyday项目——称其遵循"have your cake and eat it"的可持续发展模型，回避了问题的规模。Scenarios of Design则是"profoundly antiutopian means"（L1125），关注如何到达目标而不仅仅是描绘目标。

**论题四：时间的终局意识**

"Modern times ended before the century that announced them was over. Postmodernism was no more than an interregnum. We are now in time's endgame"（L1135）。Fry以格言式的语言宣告了当代的"终局"意识。

## 四、关键引文

> "A great deal of the future is delimited by what we have already thrown into it." (L1063)

> "We are now in time's endgame – and subject to our action it could be short or long." (L1135)
