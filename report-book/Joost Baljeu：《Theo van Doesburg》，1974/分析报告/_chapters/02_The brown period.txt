## The brown period 1902–9 Van Doesburg's earliest known painting dates from 1899. His first period, lasting from 1902–9, was of realistic paintings, chiefly portraits, some of which use a typical Rembrandtesque atmosphere of light-dark contrasts, thus revealing his activities as a copyist in the Rijksmuseum. Others employ an impressionistic or, subsequently, an expressionistic manner (from 1906 on).
