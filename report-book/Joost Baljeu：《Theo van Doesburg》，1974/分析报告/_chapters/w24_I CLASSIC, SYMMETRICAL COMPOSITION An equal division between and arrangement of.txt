## I CLASSIC, SYMMETRICAL COMPOSITION An equal division between and arrangement of halves flanking the central axis of a plane (for example, The Three Graces). Previous to the Christian era, painters often deviated from the method of symmetrical composition; after Christ, however, when thefigurative' centre, Isis, Apollo and other such subjects were replaced by Mary or Christ, the symmetrically‘centred'type of composition achieved a formal, methodical significance which lasted until the beginning of the twentieth century.
