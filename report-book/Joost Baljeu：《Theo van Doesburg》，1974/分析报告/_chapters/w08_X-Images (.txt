## X-Images (1920) (I. K. Bonset) the old Europe. It has imprisoned itself within those ideas, and it cannot liberate itself. The situation is fatal. We are watching this situation quietly; even if it were in our power to intervene, we would not. We do not desire to prolong the life of this old prostitute. A new Europe already is arising thanks to us. The first, second and third Socialist Internationals constituted ridiculous nonsense; they were merely words. The International of the Mind is an inner experience which cannot be translated into words. It does not consist of a torrent of vocables but of plastic creative acts and inner or intellectual force, which thus creates a newly shaped world. We do not summon the various nations to unite’ or to join us’. We do not incite nations to anything. We know that everybody who joins us belongs to the new. The spiritual substance of the new world will be modelled by them alone. Čreate!
