## XV Light and colour More than ever light and colour have become basic elements of architecture. Light creates space, and colour is one of the elementary means of rendering visible the harmony of architectural relationships. Without colour, relationships of proportion cannot be visualized; through colour or through contrasts of ‘denaturalized’ materials (such as glass, nickel, ebonite and aluminium) architecture in the future will attain the perfection within the contemporary style, which signifies the synthesis of all our research.
