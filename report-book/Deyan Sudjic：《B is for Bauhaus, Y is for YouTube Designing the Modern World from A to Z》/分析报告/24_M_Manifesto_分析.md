# M is for Manifesto（宣言）分析报告

## 一、章节定位与功能
本章以"Manifesto"为关键词，把建筑宣言作为一种写作类型进行分析，以Le Corbusier《走向新建筑》与Rem Koolhaas的写作实践为两座高峰。

### L### 核心论题
宣言是建筑师"以词语和建筑同时表达自己"的手段。但在后现代怀疑主义之后，宣言形式的确定性已不可能——Koolhaas的"retrospective manifesto"是这一困境的策略性回应。

### L### 关键论点
**论点一：宣言曾是建筑师的"必需品"**
> "For an architect aiming to make a mark, a manifesto is not quite the essential prop that it once was."

**论点二：Le Corbusier的宣言至今激起愤怒**
> "Simon Jenkins...claimed that 'the cruel brutalism of Le Corbusier must have caused more human misery than any other in history.'"

**论点三：Koolhaas是Le Corbusier的"转世"**
> "Koolhaas gives the impression that he believes he is the reincarnation of Le Corbusier."

**论点四：Koolhaas的立场 = "Savonarola in a Prada suit"**

**论点五："购物"（Shopping）是Koolhaas眼中吞噬一切的力量**
> "The best metaphor for shopping is that of a dying animal - a dying elephant that in its death struggle becomes completely wild and uncontrollable."

### L### 关键案例
- Le Corbusier《Vers une architecture》——Farman Goliath双翼飞机 vs. Notre-Dame
- "A house is a machine for living" / "Architecture is the masterful, correct and magnificent play of volumes brought together in light"
- Koolhaas《Delirious New York》——"retrospective manifesto"
- Koolhaas《S, M, L, XL》——包括日本色情作品、柏林墙
- Koolhaas对Tate Modern的攻击——"large spiders in the humungous conversion offer delirium for the masses"
- CCTV北京火灾——"firework display...set it on fire, killing an undisclosed number of people"
- Koolhaas被LACMA解雇——6个月
