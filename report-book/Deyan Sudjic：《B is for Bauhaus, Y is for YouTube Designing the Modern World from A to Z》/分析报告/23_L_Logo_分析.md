# L is for Logo（标志）分析报告

## 一、章节定位与功能
本章以"Logo"为关键词，探讨标识设计的符号经济学——从早期基督教鱼符号到当代奥林匹克"品牌警察"。

### L### 核心论题
Logo是"the contemporary means by which complex meanings and identities can be distilled into graphic symbols"——它不是装饰，而是价值的炼金术。

### L### 关键论点
**论点一：Logo的历史可以追溯到史前**
> "The early scripts, cuneiform, Egyptian hieroglyphs or Chinese characters, began as systems of pictograms."

**论点二：成功Logo的"化体论"**
> "The Coca-Cola bottle is both a logo and a product. With Levi jeans, you wear the logo. This is the graphic designer's version of transubstantiation."

**论点三：奥林匹克成为词语的私有化者**
> "An outrageous set of rules was put in place to prevent the use not only of the Olympic rings, but also of the words 'Olympic', 'London' and '2012'."

**论点四：Logo的寿命超越了公司名称**
> "Names may change, but logos linger."

**论点五：最值钱的Logo最初成本最低**
> "It is the logos that are now counted as among the most valuable which cost the least to create in the first place."

### L### 关键案例
- Margaret Thatcher用手帕盖住BA模型的"ethnic tail fin"
- Ford以蓝色椭圆Logo作为\$23.5 billion贷款的抵押
- Royal Mail→Consignia——"no brand could have carried more implicit value than that of the Royal Mail"
- 英国铁路双箭头——铁路旅行的通用标志
- British Airways speed bird——70年持续使用的Logo
- 水果命名潮流——Apple / Apricot / Tangerine / Orange / BlackBerry
