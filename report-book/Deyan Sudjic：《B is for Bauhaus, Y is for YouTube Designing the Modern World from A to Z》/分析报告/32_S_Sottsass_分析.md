# S is for Sottsass（埃托雷·索特萨斯）分析报告

## 一、章节定位与功能
本章以Ettore Sottsass为关键词，通过这位跨越现代主义与后现代主义的意大利设计师的生平，讲述意大利设计如何"重新定义"了现代性——"the Italians gave it a more seductive flavour, with tactile finishes, shiny plastic and saturated colours."

### L### 核心论题
Sottsass的"伟大"在于他同时在工业主流（Olivetti）和反叛边缘（Memphis）两条轨道上运作——"It was this combination of a taste for poetry with the ability to work in the industrial mainstream that set Sottsass apart."

### L### 关键论点
**论点一：意大利重新定义了现代设计**
> "If modern design had its beginnings in Britain...and was codified in Germany by the Bauhaus, it was Italy that redefined it in the 1960s."

**论点二：Sottsass的战争经历**
> "Sottsass was a lieutenant in one of the elite Alpini regiments that Mussolini sent to invade Montenegro." / 画下黑山妇女挎包的二十多种颜色 / 战后被指认为法西斯分子

**论点三：Elea 9003——第一台意大利计算机，Sottsass设计**
> "What should a computer look like?...Not like a washing machine."

**论点四：Valentine打字机——让办公设备变成个人物品**
> "a bright red plastic body, contrasted with two vivid splashes of orange on the twin ribbon spools"

**论点五：Memphis="a joyous, un-bossy manifesto for design as an emotional expression"**

**论点六：Sottsass使"艺术vs.设计"的区分变得无关紧要**
> "It was perhaps the greatest achievement of Sottsass's long and remarkable career that he made this distinction irrelevant."

### L### 关键案例
- Sottsass的战时绘画——Montenegro地毯的水彩记录
- Nanda Pivano的裙子颜色——30年后仍用作设计色标
- Elea 9003——"the size of a living room"，操作员不是被机器"吞没"
- Valentine打字机——红色塑料+橙色线轴
- Memphis——Bob Dylan / Elvis / Ancient Egypt三层命名来源
- Helmut Newton为Sottsass的房子拍照——两人"gently sparring for dominance"
- 中国人民解放军的golf resort设计
