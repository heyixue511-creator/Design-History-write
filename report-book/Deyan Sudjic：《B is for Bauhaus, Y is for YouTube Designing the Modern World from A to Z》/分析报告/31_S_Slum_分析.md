# S is for Slum（贫民窟）分析报告

## 一、章节定位与功能
本章是全书中篇幅最长、内容最沉重的章节之一。Sudjic从Mumbai的Dharavi贫民窟出发，探讨贫民窟作为"设计问题"和"城市现实"的双重面向——它既是被清理的对象，也是值得学习的城市模型。

### L### 核心论题
"A fear of slums was an essential part of the emotional background to the development of modern architecture"——但近距离观察揭示，贫民窟不是病理学问题，而是"a messy but vital range of possibilities."

### L### 关键论点
**论点一：现代建筑始于对贫民窟的恐惧**
> "Modernity was the means to eradicate slums and replace them with a utopian ideal of what the city ought to be."

**论点二：Jane Jacobs揭示"治疗比疾病更糟"**
> "Many of these cures had results that were as bad, if not worse, than the disease they claimed to have cured."

**论点三：Dharavi的figure-ground平面图与东京Shinjuku几乎相同**
> "Put such a drawing of Dharavi side by side with one of Shinjuku...the black blocks sitting on white ground split apart by white cracks and pathways are apparently all but identical. Yet one is a reflection of a world of plenty, the other of poverty."

**论点四：城市作为"脱贫机器"的功能可能并非确定**
> "But can one be quite so certain of that essentially positivist view, faced with the realities of life on the pavement?"

**论点五：我们能提供什么？**
> "What kind of experts are we, when we belong to a society that is continually demolishing housing that far surpasses anything that Mumbai's slum dwellers could ever dream of, simply because nobody wants to live in it."

### L### 关键案例
- Mumbai Hilton→Dharavi的驾车之旅——从Sir Dorab Tata Road到90 Foot Road
- Dharavi陶工——300,000人/平方公里，英属时期土地授权，抵抗拆迁
- Mumbai的tiffin wallahs——"without computers or bar codes"
- Mumbai板球场/婚礼亭——白天板球，夜晚镜面大象装饰的婚礼
- Dharavi的"中产阶级贫民窟"——每天3小时自来水，两层楼房
- 查尔斯王子参观Dharavi——"involved the construction, so it is alleged, of a special water closet"
- Kevin McCloud——"returned from Dharavi overcome by what he had seen"
- 中国vs.印度——"India is not China. It believes in open and endless debate, a free press and the rule of law."
