# H is for Habitat（家与家居）分析报告

## 一、章节定位与功能

### L### 定位
本章以"Habitat"为双重主题——既是Terence Conran创立的家居品牌名称，也是"栖息地/家居文化"的更广泛概念。通过Habitat与IKEA的对比，探讨家居设计如何塑造英国中产阶级的生活方式。

### L### 功能
- **品牌史**：Habitat从1964年创立到被IKEA体系吸收的轨迹
- **设计师论**：Terence Conran作为英国设计文化的关键代理人
- **对比分析**：Habitat（"free-range design"）vs. IKEA（"battery chicken of home-making"）
- **阶级分析**：家居风格作为社会身份的标记

## 二至三、结构与内容

### L### 核心论题
Habitat的革命在于它创造了一种"任何人都可以购买的生活方式"——"Conran style works not because people want to be like him, but because he has a knack of creating a way of life that anybody can buy into."

### L### 关键论点
**论点一：Habitat=Elizabeth David；IKEA=Delia Smith**
> "If Habitat was once the Elizabeth David of the domestic world...IKEA is more like Delia Smith. IKEA is cheaper, unavoidable, and ready to take short cuts."

**论点二：Conran的职业生涯=战后英国设计史**
> "The twists and turns of Conran's early career are the story of post-war design in Britain."

**论点三：IKEA改变了英国人对现代设计的看法**
> "IKEA ignored pretty much all the conventional wisdom about what Britain likes."

**论点四：IKEA同时摧毁和填充城市**
> "IKEA has done a lot for the way that we all live...But it has also played its part in the unravelling of the fabric of our cities."

### L### 关键案例
- Sudjic 1980年代初的Clapham厨房——全Habitat配置（软木地板→松木厨房柜→Yugoslavia榉木椅→Gitane蓝durrie地毯）
- Conran与Roy Strong的冲突——"stuffing him, putting him in a glass case and turning him into an exhibit"
- Boilerhouse Project→Design Museum
- Blair夫妇带Clintons在Pont de la Tour就餐
- IKEA英国广告——Van Den Puup (Elite Designers against IKEA)
- IKEA的命名策略——"Who cares that Moker is a ridiculous name for a desk lamp? It only costs £2.75."
