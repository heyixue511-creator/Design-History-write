# N is for National Identity（国家认同）分析报告

## 一、章节定位与功能
本章是全书中自传性最强的章节。Sudjic以个人家族移民史（从Montenegro→Bisbee, Arizona→London）和对前南斯拉夫的复杂情感作为分析框架，探讨设计如何被用于"制造国家认同"。

### L### 核心论题
"every kind of design is still put to work to achieve these ends"——从钞票、护照到建筑和展览，设计是"身份制造"的基础设施。

### L### 关键论点
**论点一：身份是被建构的**
> "The techniques by which identity is manufactured, and which constitute a key aspect of the practice of design, have always fascinated me."

**论点二：口音是最亲密的设计品**
> "Clearly an accent is an essential part of anybody's identity."

**论点三：南斯拉夫解体前的设计"预兆"——货币**
> "The Slovenes were happy to help...the Croats had devised [the kuna] to look as much like the Deutschmark as possible."

**论点四：建筑作为领土争夺的工具**
> "The Croats and the Serbs engaged in a bout of competitive church-building."

**论点五：叙事性的身份困境**
> "For my parents, both citizens of countries that had ceased to exist before they started school...the questions of identity and belonging were constant issues."

### L### 关键案例
- 南斯拉夫1000第纳尔——炼钢工人形象
- 斯洛文尼亚护照设计——Triglav山等高线地图拼贴
- Belgrade InterContinental——Tito时代的"进步"遗迹
- Belgrade飞机博物馆——从双翼飞机到F-117A stealth canopy
- 克罗地亚vs.塞尔维亚教堂建筑竞赛
- Sudjic父亲的"幽灵护照"叠层——美国→奥匈→南斯拉夫→英国
- BBC World Service——Sudjic父亲的播音职业
- Nuremberg War Crimes trial press pass——在父亲抽屉中发现
