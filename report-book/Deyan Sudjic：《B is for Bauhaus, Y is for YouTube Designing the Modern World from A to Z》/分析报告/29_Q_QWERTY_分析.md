# Q is for QWERTY（键盘）分析报告

## 一、章节定位与功能
本章是Sudjic最个人化的写作技术反思——"What you write inevitably is shaped by how you write it, or, perhaps, as anybody who has ever used predictive text will know, what you write it with."从Imperial手动打字机到Olivetti Lexikon 82到笔记本电脑，写作工具如何塑造写作内容。

### L### 核心论题
QWERTY键盘是"控制面板"——它不是中立的，它塑造了思想和语言的物质形态。"The keyboard was an essential aspect of the process of writing. It shaped and slowed the creative act."

### L### 关键论点
**论点一：QWERTY不是为了减慢打字速度而设计的——而是为了防止机械臂卡住**
> "The QWERTY keyboard was not, as is often suggested, devised to slow down typists...letters that were likely to be used in sequence, such as q and u or p and t, were placed as far apart as possible."

**论点二：Kerouac将打字机当作表演工具**
> "He wanted to avoid the disruption of being forced to stop typing, even if only for long enough to feed a new sheet of paper into his typewriter."

**论点三：语言结构可能影响储蓄行为**
> "One American academic economist has drawn a correlation between those countries that save the most as a percentage of GDP, and the grammatical structure of their language."

**论点四：QWERTY无法被取代——就像Esperanto无法取代英语**
> "Attempts to reconfigure the keyboard in supposedly more rational ways are as doomed to failure as attempts to substitute Esperanto for English."

**论点五：Olivetti Lexikon 82——"第一场对意大利设计的认真恋爱，以不愉快告终"**
> "It was the first time that I remember feeling a sense of hurt disappointment with a design that had failed to deliver."

### L### 关键案例
- Imperial手动打字机——"as heavy as a washing machine"，空间键骨折
- Kerouac的120英尺长打字纸——《On the Road》的"Dead Sea scrolls"
- Bellini的Divisumma——黄色塑料加法器，橡胶膜键盘→幻灯片的乳头图像
- IBM Selectric——"dirty pink and avocado"色彩，Eliot Noyes设计
- Olivetti Lexikon 82——Bellini设计，ABS塑料，11公斤，无法保持直线打字
- 语音识别/手写识别——"Either approach could one day finally kill off QWERTY"
