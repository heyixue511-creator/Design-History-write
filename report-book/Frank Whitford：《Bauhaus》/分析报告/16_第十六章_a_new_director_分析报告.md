# 第十六章「a new director」（新院长）分析报告

## 一、章节定位与功能

### L1600 定位

本章是**院长更替与政治化转折章**，覆盖1928年 Gropius 辞职、Hannes Meyer 继任及其1928-1930年任期。它是全书"从艺术教育到社会工程"这一转向的关键叙述段落，也是包豪斯政治化进程的核心章节。

### L1601 在全书中的功能

1. **解释 Gropius 辞职的原因**：九年耗竭——"ninety per cent of my work has been devoted to the defence of the school"
2. **引入包豪斯史上最具争议的人物**：Hannes Meyer——马克思主义者、反美学功能主义者
3. **展示意识形态如何重塑课程**：社会学、政治理论、经济学进入课程——"Art strangled life everywhere"
4. **叙述经济成功与政治灾难的并行**：收入翻倍但被右翼和左翼同时攻击
5. **完成 Meyer 被迫辞职的叙述**（1930年8月）

---

## 二、核心内容（关键论点与案例）

### L1602 — Gropius 辞职（1928年1月）
- 突然、出人意料——打破了还有两年才到期的合同
- "that until now ninety per cent of my work has been devoted to the defence of the school"
- 时机似乎吉利：校舍成功、设施优良、与资助机构关系前所未有的良好
- 继任者的选择：先找了 Mies van der Rohe（拒绝），然后选了 Hannes Meyer
- 学生代表的警告："Hannes Meyer may be a splendid fellow ... But Hannes Meyer as director of the Bauhaus is a catastrophe."
- Moholy 立即辞职——Breuer、Bayer 随后离去

### L1603 — Meyer 的"建筑作为组织"理论
- 1928年在 Bauhaus 杂志第4期发表：
- "All things on this earth are a product of the formula: (function times economy)"
- "building is a biological process. Building is not an aesthetic process"
- "architecture which produces effects introduced by the artist has no right to exist"
- "the architect? He was an artist and is becoming a specialist in organization"
- "building is only organization: social, technical, economic, mental organization."

### L1604 — Meyer 对包豪斯既有方向的攻击（1940年回顾）
- "a school whose reputation greatly exceeded its ability to achieve anything"
- "a 'Cathedral of Socialism' in which a medieval cult was pursued"
- "Incestuous theories blocked every means of designing in a way that was right for life: the cube was trumps and its faces were yellow, red, blue, white, grey, black."
- "One sat and slept on the coloured geometry of the furniture. One lived in the coloured sculpture of the houses."
- "Art strangled life everywhere."（艺术处处扼杀了生活。）

### L1605 — Meyer 的改革
- Klee 和 Kandinsky 首次被允许在课程中教授绘画
- Schlemmer 被任命为教授——开设新课"Man"（人）：人体写生+生物学+哲学讲座
- 常规体操课程引入
- Hilbersheimer 教城市规划
- 客座讲师带来社会学、马克思主义政治理论、物理学、工程学、心理学、经济学
- 新摄影工作坊在 Walter Peterhans 领导下成立（他原是数学家）
- 工坊被要求生产廉价、批量生产的基本物品——"not supplying the luxury market"
- 学生被给予实际问题解决：Törten 住宅区最后阶段的建造、Bernau 工会学校

### L1606 — 经济成功的讽刺
- 壁纸设计（Rasch 公司）："In the first year alone four-and-a-half million rolls were sold."
- 纺织和家具工坊也为学校赚取了可观资金
- 广告部赢得 I.G. Farben 的合同——设计所有报纸广告
- 学校首次有可能实现自给自足——"under the Marxist Meyer benefited enormously from the success of the capitalist system"
- 入学人数从160增至197——"Only by introducing entrance qualifications were we able to keep the numbers down."

### L1607 — 政治灾难
- 共产主义小组成立——15名成员，约占学生的10%
- 1930年狂欢节派对：学生唱俄国革命歌曲——"had not been well received"
- 右翼媒体散播谣言
- 市长 Hesse 被指控用市政美术馆资金购买包豪斯教师画作装饰自己房间
- Kandinsky 参与罢免 Meyer 的密谋
- Gropius 从远处"保持仁慈的目光"——也赞成尽快换人
- 1930年 Meyer 被迫辞职——表面上是因以学校名义向罢工矿工捐款
- 公开信致市长："he had been 'stabbed in the back'"
- 前往苏联——"I am going to the USSR in order to work where a truly proletarian culture is being forged"

### L1608 — 双重夹击
- 包豪斯现在左右皆敌
- 共产主义画报 AIZ："a revolutionary Bauhaus was an illusion in a capitalist state"
- "In becoming the target of radicals from both ends of the spectrum the school continued to mirror the predicament of the Weimar Republic"
