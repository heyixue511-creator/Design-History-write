He then worked on the Estrées viaduct project for two years and on theManicouagan dam project in Québec from 196oto 1963. He designed a prototype for a small urban car in 1964 that was essentially a clear acrylic cuboidwith wheels, and three years later hisQuasar Unipower cars were produced in small numbers. Along with other French designers, includingRonald-Cecil Sportes (b. 1943)and Bernard Quentin (b. 1923), he then began to explore the potential ofinflatable structures, which were promoted by the"Les StructuresGonflables" exhibition staged at ARC in 1967, designing a series of inflatable furniture pieces in 1966 and a small circular inflatable dwelling in 1968. During the late 196os,Quasar sponsored Philippe Starck to design several more inflatable structures.
