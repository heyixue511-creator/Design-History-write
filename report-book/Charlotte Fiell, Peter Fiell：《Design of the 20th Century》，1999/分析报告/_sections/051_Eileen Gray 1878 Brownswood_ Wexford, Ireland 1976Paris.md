From193o to 1931, she planned the interior of Badovici's apartmentin Rue Chateaubriand, followed by another house for her own use, theTempe a Pailla at Castellar, completed in 1934. After exhibiting her work at Le Corbusier's Pavillondes Temps Nouveaux in 1937, and presenting an unrealized proposal for a Centre de Vacances, Gray faded into virtual obscurity until 197o, when an American collector,Robert Walker, began buyingup her designs, initiating a significant revival of interest in her life andwork. ##
