## 一、章节定位与功能

### L1 章节目录信息
- **设计师**：René Lalique（1860-1945）
- **位置**：Catalogue第二十一个条目
- **藏品编号**：no.27 — Monnaie du pape Coffer / no.28 — Hagueneau Wine Glasses / no.29 — Tourbillons Vase / no.30 — Vase / no.31 — Rings and Necklace
- **规模**：传记约6页 + 5组藏品描述（全书条目最多的设计师之一）

### L2 功能定位
Lalique是**全书中最知名的设计师**——"probably the best-known figure of French Art Deco"——也是唯一"fully embrace industrial production"的设计师。他代表了Art Deco中艺术与工业大规模生产的和解。

### L3 特殊性
Lalique是唯一运营着数百名工人、两个大工厂、国际分销网络、产品覆盖从建筑构件到香水瓶的全谱系设计师。他的独特之处在于将**工业制造转化为奢侈品牌**的能力。

## 三、内容分析

### L1 核心论题

**工业生产的艺术化**：
> "his particular skill as a designer is what allowed him to transform factory-made glass into a desirable luxury."

Lalique证明了工业制造不必牺牲审美品质。与Dunand的"I refuse to manufacture"构成全书最尖锐的立场对比。

**"民主又贵族"的艺术观**：
> "This is at once the most democratic and the most aristocratic view of art. It assumes that all objects associated with human experience are available for the expression of beauty."

门把手、刀叉、"我们最普通时刻的工具"都应该美丽——这是Art Deco最激进也最平易近人的宣言。

**无色玻璃的纯粹性**：
> "he has not made that unfortunate error of using color for a wine glass. A gourmet takes pleasure in admiring the robe [color] of the wine he will be enjoying."

Gaston Derys为Lalique只用无色玻璃做酒杯辩护——欣赏酒的"robe"（色泽袍服）是品酒仪式的一部分。

### L2 关键案例
- **Monnaie du pape Coffer（1914）**：玻璃"保险柜"——"what sort of strongbox is made of glass?"材料与功能的刻意错配（视觉双关：Lunaria植物="教皇钱币"→存钱箱）
- **Hagueneau Wine Glasses（1924）**：量产餐具→Alsace地区命名→六种杯型→\$78/打（相当于今天\$1,300）→Steuben类似产品\$90-108/打→价格竞争力
- **Tourbillons Vase（ca.1926）**：黑色珐琅边缘+无色玻璃+漩涡纹→"strong patterning of swirling lines... lends a dynamism that stands alone in his work"→可能是女儿Suzanne Lalique的初始设计
- **Cire-perdue Vase（no.30）**：失蜡法→每件独特→极高破损风险→1919年评论："appalling amount of risk"→至1933年共650+件

## 七、语言文风

### L1 原文摘录
> "He has dared to introduce his art into those departments of life which we are too often content to furnish with machine-made conveniences. He sees no reason why the doorknobs we turn, the knives and forks we use, the tools or instruments of our most ordinary moments should not be beautiful."

> "In contradistinction to the many glassworkers who today are running riot in bold colour, Lalique employs this very little... This reticence in use of all colouration, throws into fine relief the quality of his modelling and the grace of shape."

> "Naturally, he has not made that unfortunate error of using color for a wine glass."
