# X is for Xerox（施乐/柯达）分析报告

## 一、章节定位与功能
本章以Kodak和Xerox这对"诞生于同一个New York上州城市的五字母品牌"为比较案例，讲述两个公司在数字革命中截然不同的命运——Kodak破产，Xerox幸存。

### L### 核心论题
"Neither Kodak nor Xerox...still have the name that they were born with."——制造影像和复制技术的公司如何应对数字化浪潮，揭示了关于创新、品牌和企业文化的深层真相。

### L### 关键论点
**论点一：Kodak曾经等同于"民主化摄影"**
> "Eastman took processing exposed film, and printing of the result, out of the hands of the photographer. He turned photography from a special interest into a universal pastime."

**论点二：Kodak的商业模式70年没有根本变化——直到数字革命**
> "The cameras may have looked different...but there was no substantial technological shift or change in the business model in seventy years."

**论点三：从巅峰到破产仅15年**
> "At its peak, in 1997...the company was valued at \$30 billion. Fifteen years later, Kodak was bankrupt."

**论点四：Xerox幸存因为它不把自己定位为"复印机公司"**
> "Xerox had always invested heavily in research. It was working out how to connect its copying machines with copper telephone lines in the early 1960s."

**论点五：Xerox PARC发明了个人电脑的所有元素——但从未商业化**
> "Xerox's scientists put all the elements of the forerunner of the personal computer together, with a QWERTY keyboard, a cathode ray screen, the graphic user interface and the first usable mouse."

**论点六：Steve Jobs vs. Bill Gates的"富邻居Xerox"比喻**
> "'I think it's more like we both had this rich neighbour named Xerox, and I broke into his house to steal the TV set, and found out that you had already stolen it.'"

### L### 关键案例
- George Eastman发明"Kodak"名字——"It is short. It is not capable of mispronunciation."
- 1900 Kodak Brownie→1963 Instamatic→Sudjic的圣诞礼物
- 2000年：美国购买9.5亿卷胶卷→2010年：不到1亿
- Kodak为Apple制造QuickTake数字相机（1994年）——然后被Apple甩掉
- Xerox 914（1959年）——"made modern bureaucracy possible"——也为激进学生提供复印传单的手段
- Chester Carlson的"Xeroxography"——希腊文"干"+"写"
- Xerox PARC——GUI, mouse, QWERTY interface

### L### 实体清单
**人物**：George Eastman / Chester Carlson / Steve Jobs / Bill Gates / Walter Dorwin Teague / Raymond Loewy / Charles & Ray Eames
**机构**：Eastman Kodak / Xerox (Haloid) / Xerox PARC / Apple / Microsoft / Fuji / Gillette
**物质对象**：Kodak Brownie / Instamatic / Kodachrome slide projector / Xerox 914 / Apple QuickTake
**地点**：Rochester, NY / Palo Alto, CA
**概念**：Digital disruption / "We do the rest" / Dry copying / GUI / Patent armoury
