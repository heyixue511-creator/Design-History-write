# Y is for YouTube（YouTube与数字设计）分析报告

## 一、章节定位与功能
本章以YouTube为关键词，将"数字界面设计"纳入设计批评的范畴。YouTube代表了"a new version of designer, one who dealt in the nonphysical world as much as the physical one"——Chad Hurley的PayPal button"may have been the most valuable switch ever made."

### L### 核心论题
数字世界的"设计"已经从物理形态转向系统设计——"a glowing image on a screen...represents a far more complex set of mechanical and formal issues than any door handle, keyboard or switch."

### L### 关键论点
**论点一：从物理设计到数字设计的代际转变**
> "Jonathan Ive, born ten years before Hurley, is old enough to have approached design from a physical point of view...Hurley was born too late to have a professional engagement with the analogue."

**论点二：PayPal button="可能是有史以来最值钱的开关"**

**论点三：YouTube不做特定用途的预设**
> "Hurley decided not to make it too specific. YouTube evolved into a medium that could be used for almost anything...Its function was closer to printing than it was to being an alphabet."

**论点四：UGC社区视频的幻想——"crudely edited community video channels were never any kind of threat to the BBC"**

**论点五：YouTube没有目录——"popularity"是唯一的组织原则**
> "It is a phenomenon that runs counter to the notional democracy of YouTube...The culture of YouTube is tilted irreversibly in directions that tend to reinforce existing tastes rather than challenge them."

**论点六：价值创造≠就业创造**
> "When YouTube was acquired by Google, it had less than 100 employees, a stark reminder that while technology was creating value, it wasn't doing much to create jobs."

**论点七：Sudjic的YouTube"表现"**
> "No talk, no post, nothing that I have done has ever clocked up more than 6,000 views. By the standards of YouTube, where millions of people see the same images in hours, that is less than invisible."

### L### 关键案例
