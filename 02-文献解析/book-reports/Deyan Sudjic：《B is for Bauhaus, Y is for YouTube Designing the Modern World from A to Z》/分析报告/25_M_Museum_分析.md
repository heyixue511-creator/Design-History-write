# M is for Museum（博物馆）分析报告

## 一、章节定位与功能
本章以Guggenheim Museum为案例，剖析博物馆从"珍宝库"到"城市更新引擎+娱乐综合体+全球品牌"的转型。

### L### 核心论题
"the museum into the most flamboyant building type of the first two decades of the twenty-first century"——Guggenheim作为"特别花哨的博物馆自我主义案例"。

### L### 关键论点
**论点一：Krens的Guggenheim = "global art circus"**
> "In seventeen years at the Guggenheim, Krens turned a modestly scaled museum...into a global art circus, positioned conceptually somewhere between a casino and a department store."

**论点二：Hilla Rebay是Guggenheim的真正创始人**
> "Krens suggested to me that Rebay got Guggenheim's name from the passenger lists for the transatlantic sailings."

**论点三：Frank Lloyd Wright vs. James Johnson Sweeney**
> "Sweeney despaired of Wright's spiralling, sloping walls as a place in which to show serious art."

**论点四：Krens售卖馆藏的争议**
> "Krens sold three paintings, a Modigliani for \$10 million, Kandinsky's Fugue...for \$20.9 million."

**论点五：Guggenheim的全球化模式失败了**
> "Krens had gambled that a worldwide network of museums would allow him to spread exhibition costs...it didn't work."

### L### 关键案例
- Rebay与Guggenheim的初次会面——"like that scene from Basic Instinct when Sharon Stone uncrosses her legs"
- Rebay建议Wright拔掉牙齿以获精神健康——"We have sacrificed enough for this commission"
- Peggy Guggenheim vs. Rebay——"Nazi witch"
- Sweeney发明白墙极简博物馆——与Wright/Rebay的理念完全相反
- Bilbao Guggenheim——\$20 million费用+惊人宣传
- Las Vegas Guggenheim——"opened and shut after a humiliatingly short life"
- Armani展览——\$15 million赞助引发"尊严"争议
- 21位艺术家联名抗议Wright建筑——"We shall be compelled to paint to suit Mr Wright's architecture"
