# P is for Postmodern（后现代主义）分析报告

## 一、章节定位与功能
本章以Charles Jencks 1977年宣告"现代建筑于1972年7月15日下午3点32分在圣路易斯死亡"的著名句子为起点，追踪后现代主义的崛起与迅速衰落。它是全书对现代主义谱系最集中的论述。

### L### 核心论题
后现代主义从"对现代主义的欢乐叛乱"到"自身也死亡了"的轨迹——"If modernism was dead, and postmodernism was manifestly even deader, what was left?"

### L### 关键论点
**论点一：Jencks捏造了精确时间——但那不重要**
> "What did it matter, as Jencks later admitted, that he had made up the killer detail that pinned the time of the blast down to the precise minute?"

**论点二：现代主义=一种道德立场，不仅仅是风格**
> "To be a modernist was not merely about making things look fashionably up to date. It was to have a point of view about everything from music to psychoanalysis."

**论点三：后现代主义=现代主义+法国文学理论**
> "Postmodernism was modernism plus French literary theory."

**论点四：后现代在V&A回顾展时已是一具安全的尸体**
> "By the time the Victoria and Albert Museum got around to its retrospective on postmodernism, forty years later, it was a moment in design history that seemed as though it had well and truly passed."

**论点五：Jencks发明了"新范式"——后现代的续集**
> "According to Jencks, 'the New Paradigm' is the next big thing for architecture."

### L### 关键案例
- Pruitt-Igoe爆破——"Boom Boom Boom"
- Venturi:"Less is a bore"
- Michael Graves——从"自Frank Lloyd Wright以来最重要的美国建筑师"到"swan-topped Disney hotels"
- Venturi & Scott Brown Knoll家具（1984）——Chippendale/Sheraton/Empire/Art Deco"引用"
- Daniel Weil的塑料袋收音机
- Harry Seidler——"postmodernism was the architectural equivalent of AIDS"
- Paul Rand对后现代的吐槽——"collage of confusion and chaos"
- 查尔斯王子——"the Luftwaffe...did less damage to London than Britain's modern architects"
- Graves被解雇——从创新标志到"terminally out of touch"
