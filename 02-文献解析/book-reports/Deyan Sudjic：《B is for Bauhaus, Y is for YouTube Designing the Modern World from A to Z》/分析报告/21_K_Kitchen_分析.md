# K is for Kitchen（厨房）分析报告

## 一、章节定位与功能

### L### 定位
本章以"Kitchen"为关键词，通过Margarethe Schütte-Lihotzky的生平与法兰克福厨房（Frankfurt Kitchen）的设计史，探讨现代主义如何渗透到最私密的家庭空间，以及厨房如何从"阶级战争的战场"转变为"家庭生活的象征中心"。

### L### 功能
- **社会史**：厨房作为社会阶级关系的战场（主人vs.仆人）
- **设计师论**：Schütte-Lihotzky——共产主义革命建筑师，活了100岁
- **性别政治**：厨房作为"女性空间"的现代转型
- **消费批判**：当代厨房作为"最大的阶级标志物"

## 二至三、结构与内容

### L### 核心论题
"the kitchen has become the symbolic centre of the home in a way that it never used to be"——但Schütte-Lihotzky的初衷是效率与解放，而非消费主义。

### L### 关键论点
**论点一：早期厨房是"阶级战争的战场"**
> "In the early twentieth century the middle-class kitchen was at the front line of a particularly bitter variety of class warfare."

**论点二：Schütte-Lihotzky将Taylorism应用于家务劳动**
> "She was influenced by the theories of Taylorism...and by his determination to lay the production line out so efficiently that the worker became an integral part of it."

**论点三：厨房是技术进入家庭的门户**
> "The kitchen was the route through which electrical and mechanical appliances started filtering into the home."

**论点四：当代厨房变成了奢侈品消费中心**
> "It is precisely the combination of the domestic ideal and Schütte-Lihotzky's celebration of efficiency that has made the kitchen the centre of the domestic world."

### L### 关键案例
- Schütte-Lihotzky的100岁生日——"Waiters in white gloves dispensed Sekt and Sachertorte, while she took to the floor to dance a waltz."
- 法兰克福厨房——8000套安装在工人住宅区
- 浴缸带盖——兼作额外工作台面
- Electrolux的El Bulli家用版——£250,000
- 附属厨房（preparation kitchen）——"One for the staff, and one for their employers"

**机构**：Vienna Academy of Applied Art / Museum für Angewandte Kunst

**地点**：Frankfurt / Vienna / Magnitogorsk / Cuba / Istanbul
**概念**：Taylorism / Built-in kitchen / Galley kitchen / Time-and-motion study
