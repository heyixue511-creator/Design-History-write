# T is for Taste（品味）分析报告

## 一、章节定位与功能
本章系统处理"品味"作为设计社会学的核心概念。从Kant→Simmel→Bourdieu的阶级品味理论，到James Laver的时尚时间图表，再到Sudjic自著《Cult Objects》的自我批判，本章揭示了品味作为"阶级信号系统"的多层运作。

### L### 核心论题
"Making sense of taste represents a certain challenge for intellectuals. They understand the subject in class terms. They understand, of course, that they themselves are a class."

### L### 关键论点
**论点一：品味=阶级信号**
> "In the Kant-to-Simmel scheme of things, ruling classes set fashions and create tastes, while the rest of the pack struggle to catch up."

**论点二：Reyner Banham的bolo tie=品味的精心否定**
> "By adopting it, Banham was carefully signalling both that he knew a lot about the world of taste, and that he did not care to be part of it."

**论点三：Loos关于"得体着装"的定义**
> "The point is to be dressed in such a manner as to attract as little attention as possible."

**论点四：Simmel的Kaffirs/Bushmen比较——缺乏阶级=缺乏时尚**
> "The Bushmen...who have developed no class-system, have no fashions whatsoever."

**论点五：品味信号可以双向流动（自下而上）**
> "Some affluent middle-class schoolchildren in London will adopt the speech rhythms of the children of Caribbean migrants."

**论点六：Laver的时间法则—50年周期**
> "It took ten years for the indecent to move through the shameless and the outré stages to become smart."

**论点七：Sudjic对自己旧书的忏悔**
> "Looking back at Cult Objects now, I see that it was actually more confessional than analytical."

### L### 关键案例
- Banham的bolo tie——Teddy boy→美国西部保守政客→建筑历史学家
- Burberry——从Hampshire店主→军官壕沟大衣→足球流氓邪教→Christopher Bailey复兴
- Casual亚文化——英国球迷在1980年代的欧洲赛场上发现Lacoste/Fila
- Alexander McQueen——监狱低腰裤→公立学校男孩
- Henry Cole的V&A"坏设计"展览——最受欢迎的展品
- Stephen Bayley的Boilerhouse "Taste"展——良好品味的物品放在底座上，坏品味的放在垃圾桶上→Terry Farrell抢回他的TV-am模型
- 《Cult Objects》封面——"Do you speak Burberry?"
