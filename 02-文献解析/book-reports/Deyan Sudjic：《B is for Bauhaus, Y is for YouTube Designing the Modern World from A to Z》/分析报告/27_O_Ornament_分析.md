# O is for Ornament（装饰）分析报告

## 一、章节定位与功能
本章以Loos的"Ornament and Crime"为核心文本，探讨装饰在现代化过程中的地位变迁——从"文明的标志"到"犯罪"再到当代的"功能性托词"和"个人化纹身"。

### L### 核心论题
"Ornament is anything but superficial. It's a deadly serious business: the means with which cultures and individuals define themselves."

### L### 关键论点
**论点一：装饰曾经是工匠的个人签名**
> "Ornament was once the way that individual craftsmen could make their personal contribution to a larger idea."

**论点二：Owen Jones的经典公式**
> "Construction should be decorated. Decoration should never be purposely constructed."

**论点三：Loos的论点被误解了——他不是反装饰，而是反Hoffmann式的"假装成艺术的装饰"**

**论点四：当代装饰="功能性托词"**
> "For contemporary mass-produced domestic appliances, decoration is almost exclusively based on functional alibis."

### L### 关键案例
- Loos vs. Hoffmann——维也纳内部的文化战争
- Wiener Werkstätte——"making work for the daughters of the rich"
- Owen Jones《The Grammar of Ornament》
- Christopher Dresser的1879年茶壶——纯几何形态，60年后看起来仍然新颖
- Loos的"穷人小富翁"——对van de Velde的讽刺
- Pompidou Centre彩色管道——"decorated construction"
- 汽车挡风玻璃圆点图案——视觉装饰的"功能托词"
- Dyson吸尘器和iBook的"功能色彩"
- 豹纹手机壳——"we tattoo our possessions as well as ourselves"
