# 28 章节分析报告：留白（White Space）

## 一、章节定位与功能

本章是全书技术部分的**美学与哲学高峰**。"留白"是 Vignelli 排版哲学中最核心的概念——它在 Contrasting Type Sizes 章中被首次深入讨论，在此章中获得**独立成章的全面展开**。留白不仅是技术手段，更是一种文化认同——Vignelli 将美国设计传统中的留白运用视为其最独特的贡献。

## 三、内容分析

### L1 核心论题

**在排版中，留白比黑色的文字更重要。留白在印刷页面上等同于空间在建筑中——空间赋予了语境的品质。留白不仅分隔信息的不同部分，更有助于将信息定位在页面的语境中。紧凑的边距在文本与页面边缘之间建立张力；更宽的边距消解张力并带来一定程度的宁静。对留白的高超处理也许是美国平面设计最独特的属性——就像空间是 Frank Lloyd Wright 建筑中的主角。**

### L2 关键论点

| 论点 | 原文依据 |
|------|---------|
| "在排版中，留白比黑色的文字更重要" | "the white space is more important than the black of the type." |
| "留白在印刷页面上=空间在建筑中" | "The white space on the printed page is the correspondent of space in architecture." |
| 留白不仅分隔信息部分，还帮助定位信息在页面中的位置 | "White space, non only separates the different parts of the message but helps to position the message in the context of the page." |
| 紧凑边距=张力；宽边距=宁静 | "Tight margins establish a tension...Wider margins deflate the tension and bring about a certain level of serenity to the page." |
| 紧凑字距将词转化为线；松散字距将词转化为点 | "Tight type setting transforms words into lines just as loose type settings transform words in to dots." |
| 字偶距（kerning）的增减赋予文字独特的性格和表达 | "Decreasing or increasing the letter spacing (kerning) confers very distinctive character and expression to the words." |
| "对留白的高超处理也许是美国平面设计最独特的属性" | "the masterful handling of white space on a printed page is perhaps the most peculiar attribute of American graphic design." |

## 七、语言文风

### L1 原文摘录

> **L7.1** "I often say that in typography the white space is more important than the black of the type."

> **L7.2** "The white space on the printed page is the correspondent of space in architecture. In both situations space is what qualifies the context."

> **L7.3** "Tight margins establish a tension between text, images and the edges of the page. Wider margins deflate the tension and bring about a certain level of serenity to the page."

> **L7.4** "the masterful handling of white space on a printed page is perhaps the most peculiar attribute of American graphic design. Just like space is the protagonist in Frank Lloyd Wright's architecture. Somehow, it relates to the epic grandeur of the American landscapes."

> **L7.5** "Almost all the great American graphic designers have used white space as the significant silence to better hear their message loud and clear. Such is indeed the power of the white space."
