# 07_第七章分析报告：二十世纪晚期——T台与街头风格（Late Twentieth Century: Catwalk and Street Style）

## L### 一、章节定位与功能

本章是全书时间线的末端，聚焦于1970年代末至1990年代中期的时尚文化，以"catwalk and street style"（T台与街头风格）这对对立/互补的概念为核心框架。Breward在开篇坦承时间距离太近带来的史学困难——"it is perhaps difficult to place contemporary fashion under the same kind of 'objective' scrutiny as the clothing of the more distant past"——但坚持认为对当代时尚的审视既"强调了历史和文化知识可以被付诸实践和创造性的用途"，也是全书的"恰当且具有潜在帮助的结论"。

章节的特殊功能在于：它不仅是对1978-1995年时尚风格的描述，更是对1980年代"设计师十年"（designer decade）这一文化建构的解剖——通过引用Nicholas Coleridge、Guy Debord、Iain Chambers等人的理论，Breward将当代时尚定位为"spectacle"（奇观）的终极体现。

## L### 二、结构分析

本章篇幅相对短小（约27000字符），较前几章更为精简，结构如下：

1. **开篇引子**：以Nicholas Coleridge《The Fashion Conspiracy》（1988）的"阴谋论"修辞开场，同时既承认又解构"1980年代代表了一场时尚革命"的叙事
2. **"Spectacle"作为分析框架**：引入Guy Debord的"spectacle"（奇观）概念——"The spectacle is not a collection of images...but a social relationship among people, mediated by images"——作为理解当代时尚的核心概念
3. **"Lifestyle"话语**：分析Tony & Claes Lewenhaupt《Crosscurrents》等"生活方式"出版物如何将当代时尚编码为一种关于身份和消费的文化速记
4. **设计师个案分析**：
   - **Rei Kawakubo (Comme des Garçons)**：被Deyan Sudjic包装为"现代主义先驱"——"an echo of the purism of the pioneering architectural modernists"
   - **Zandra Rhodes**：从1960年代起将伦敦bohemian和街头风格融入高级时装
   - **Vivienne Westwood**：作为"street and catwalk"二元对立的超越者——"Westwood has continually found herself not simply reacting to current trends, but delineating and defining them at the same time"
5. **John Fairchild的"商业现实"揭示**：高定时装的真正利润来自香水和配饰——"the designers started making their serious money with perfumes and accessories"
6. **结论**：Iain Chambers关于大众文化"从被忽视和被压制的领域涌现"的乌托邦式论断

## L### 三、内容分析

### 核心论题

本章的核心论题可以概括为：**在20世纪末，"时尚"作为一种文化体系已经实现了从"物质对象"到"图像/奇观"的全面转变——但这一转变并不意味着时尚失去了其作为社会差异建构者和反映者的功能。** Breward既承认Debord式的批评（"the image of a thing superseded the thing itself"），又试图超越这种批评，将"奇观"重新定位为时尚文化史的持续特征而非20世纪末独有的退化。

### 关键论点与案例

1. **"设计师十年"的修辞分析**（L### 1）：Breward选择不简单地描述80年代"时尚革命"，而是通过Coleridge的"阴谋论"修辞来展示这种叙事本身是如何被建构的——"The fashion conspiracy is not simply a conspiracy of expensive clothes...it is a conspiracy of taste and compromise." Breward指出Coleridge的"谴责性修辞"听起来与之前各章引用的反消费主义论调"惊人地相似"（sounds familiar in its similarity），从而暗示当代批评与历史先例之间的连续性。

2. **Kawakubo与"从零开始"的现代主义神话**（L### 2）：Breward对Sudjic关于Rei Kawakubo的论述进行了含蓄的批评——"she likes she has said 'to start from zero'...it is not too far fetched to see the influence of [Le Corbusier's] purist modernism in her own abstraction of fashion into the fundamentals of texture, form and colour." Breward指出这种话语是"一种故意的营销策略"（a deliberate marketing ploy），而非纯粹的美学真理。

3. **Vivienne Westwood作为超越者**（L### 3）：Westwood的kitchen-sink cardigan（由紫色洗碗棉制成、Vim清洁粉盖子作为纽扣）被用作一个核心案例——Breward引述Valerie Mendes的详细描述，展示Westwood如何将"传统的家庭和性别化的服装选择范畴"与"日本服装的形式品质"、"达达主义对现成品的挪用"以及"1980年代早期'Buffalo'青年风格"糅合在一起。Juliet Ash的名言被引用来强调Westwood的不可归类性："Westwood's work eludes the journalist...the fashion historian...the postmodernist critic...the fashion business 'rep' and the buyer."

4. **高定时装的商业真相**（L### 4）：John Fairchild, 作为《Women's Wear Daily》的编辑，揭示了一个残酷的真相——"none of the leading designers could support their studios based on couture sales alone...After all, a very simple suit in the couture can easily cost $10,000...the designers started making their serious money with perfumes and accessories." Breward引述这一点来消解"设计师作为纯粹创造天才"的神话——尽管他也在其他地方通过引述Sudjic等人的语言来参与建构这种神话。

5. **Christian Lacroix的"历史引用"**（L### 5）：Breward引述Lacroix在1988年3月《Vogue》中的话——"every one of my dresses possesses a detail that can be connected with something historic...We don't invent anything"——并将他的作品描述为"装饰性的拼贴"（decorative pastiche），与Westwood的"subversion"（颠覆）形成鲜明对比。

6. **Chambers的"流行文化乌托邦"**（L### 6）：Breward以Iain Chambers的一段近乎乌托邦式的话语结束全书——"Contemporary popular culture may no longer be strictly 'working-class'...but it does emerge from subordinate cultures, from the inventive edges of the consensus, from the previously ignored and suppressed. It gestures through a widening democratization of styles, sounds and images, to an important remaking, to new possibilities, new perspectives, new projects." 然而，考虑到全书一贯的批判性立场，这段引述应该被理解为Breward有意呈现的一种"可能性的视野"而非全书结论。

## L### 四、逻辑梳理

### 论证链条

```
1980年代被广泛描述为一场"时尚革命"（designer decade）
    ↓（但）
Coleridge的"阴谋论"和Debord的"spectacle"提供了批判框架
    ↓（考察具体案例）
1. Kawakubo → 被商业包装为"现代主义先驱"
2. Rhodes → 将街头/bohemian风格译为高级时装语言
3. Westwood → 超越了 catwalk/street 的二元对立
4. 高定时装的"真相"（Fairchild）→ 利润来自香水和配饰
    ↓（反思）
当代时尚 = 图像（spectacle）优先于物质
    ↓（但这不是全新的）
从1350年代起，时尚就已经在运作"奇观"的逻辑
    ↓（Chambers的乌托邦式结尾）
流行文化从边缘涌现，指向"新的可能性、新的视角、新的计划"
```

### 因果转折

- 设计师的"创造天才"（Kawakubo的"start from zero"）→ 被揭示为"营销策略" → 但Breward没有全盘否定（Sudjic的框架"也有道理"）
- Lacroix的历史引用 → 被贬为"装饰性拼贴" → Westwood的历史引用 → 被赞扬为"颠覆" → 区别在哪里？Breward暗示：Westwood的引用挑战了时尚的权力结构，而Lacroix的引用只是复制了它
- "奇观"（Debord）→ 通常被视为文化退化的标志 → 但Breward通过Chambers的引语暗示spectacle也可能有解放性的潜力
- 时尚体系似乎已"内爆"（implosion）→ 但Chambers代表的对"边缘"和"被压制"的强调意味着新的可能性

## L### 五、材料使用方式

1. **当代新闻话语**（Nicholas Coleridge, The Fashion Conspiracy 1988）：被既引用又解构——作为理解80年代"时尚阴谋论"话语的窗口
2. **时尚出版物的"生活方式"话语**（Tony & Claes Lewenhaupt, Crosscurrents）：被分析为将时尚编码为文化速记的典型案例
3. **设计师专著**（Deyan Sudjic, Rei Kawakubo and Comme des Garçons 1990）：被用于展示"设计师作为天才"的意识形态建构
4. **时尚记者自传**（John Fairchild, Chic Savages 1989）：提供对高定时装商业现实的内部揭示
5. **博物馆出版物**（Valerie Mendes & Claire Wilcox, Modern Fashion in Detail 1991）：提供对Westwood cardigan的精确物质描述
6. **文化理论**（Guy Debord, Society of the Spectacle; Iain Chambers, Popular Culture: The Metropolitan Experience 1986）：作为分析框架
7. **学术论文集**（Juliet Ash & Elizabeth Wilson, Chic Thrills 1992）：当代时尚学术研究的集中体现

## L### 六、论辩与阐述方法

1. **自我反思性**：Breward坦率承认对当代时尚进行"客观"审视的困难——"the historian slips swiftly into the world of journalism and celebration"
2. **双重声音**：对每个被分析的文本，Breward既是"引用者"（让文本自己说话）也是"批评者"（揭示文本的意识形态功能）
3. **当代↔历史的对照**：不断将当代现象与历史先例并置——Coleridge的修辞vs.以前各章的anti-consumption diatribes; Westwood的颠覆vs. 18世纪的molly culture; 1980年代的spectacle vs. 1350年代时尚体系的诞生
4. **个案研究深度法**：以Westwood为核心案例，给予最详细的物质和理论分析
5. **结论的开放性**：Breward以Chambers的引述而非自己的声明结束，暗示对当代时尚的最终评判尚待历史的裁决

## L### 七、语言文风

### 原文摘录

> **L### 摘录1**（Coleridge的"阴谋论"）：
> "The fashion conspiracy is not simply a conspiracy of expensive clothes being marked up around the world, it is a conspiracy of taste and compromise; the prerogative of the international fashion editors in determining how the world dresses...the despotic vanity of the designers and the ruthlessness of the store buyers...Often the conspiracy is a conspiracy of silence."

> **L### 摘录2**（Debord的spectacle）：
> "The Spectacle as Debord developed the concept through the 1950s and 1960s was...a wonderful prison where all of life was staged as a permanent show...'capital accumulated until it becomes an image'...The spectacle is not a collection of images...but a social relationship among people, mediated by images."

> **L### 摘录3**（Sudjic论Kawakubo）：
> "There is an echo of the purism of the pioneering architectural modernists about the way Kawakubo approaches fashion; she likes she has said 'to start from zero', re-examining not just the appearance of clothes but their construction."

> **L### 摘录4**（Fairchild的"真相"）：
> "Even back in my early Paris days, none of the leading designers could support their studios based on couture sales alone, for very, very few women dressed in the couture...the designers started making their serious money with perfumes and accessories."

> **L### 摘录5**（Mendes对Westwood cardigan的描述）：
> "it is made in loosely woven, dishcloth cotton, dyed purple. Its singular look is completed by three huge burnished metal buttons, which are made from 'Vim' scouring powder lids. Each lid is attached with coloured cotton thread through the ready punched holes and fastens with a loop of thick cotton cord."

> **L### 摘录6**（Chambers的乌托邦式结尾）：
> "Contemporary popular culture may no longer be strictly 'working-class' as the idealistic purists of political formalism would like, but it does emerge from subordinate cultures, from the inventive edges of the consensus, from the previously ignored and suppressed. It gestures through a widening democratization of styles, sounds and images, to an important remaking, to new possibilities, new perspectives, new projects."

### 文风特征
- 篇幅明显短于前几章（约27000字符vs Ch4的71000+），反映当代时尚研究的available source materials较少
- 引述和反引述的交替非常多——Breward经常在一个段落后立即以某种方式限定或质疑前一段的内容
- 术语密度极高——spectacle, bricolage, postmodern, deconstructivist, myth, lifestyle——反映1980-90年代文化理论的渗透
- 间接批评多于直接批评——Breward更倾向于通过引述对立文本（如Fairchild vs. Sudjic）来创造对话张力，而非自己作为裁判

7. **Subculture** — 亚文化，特别是与Westwood的punk/street文化关联
