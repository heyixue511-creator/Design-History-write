Burks (2008)。研究美国Shaker教派的家具设计如何体现其宗教信仰——'Faith, Form and Finish'三位一体。Shaker家具的简洁不是美学的选择而是神学的命令：'We are not called to be like the world; but to excel them in order, union and peace, and in good works—works that are truly virtuous and useful to man, in this life.'（源文件L8605）实体：Burks L148; Shaker furniture; faith-form-function relationship; American craft tradition L148。关联：作为Section 8的收尾，展示了'物-信仰-设计'的整合分析——呼应了Section 2中Ruskin的'哥特建筑作为基督教价值表达'的分析。
