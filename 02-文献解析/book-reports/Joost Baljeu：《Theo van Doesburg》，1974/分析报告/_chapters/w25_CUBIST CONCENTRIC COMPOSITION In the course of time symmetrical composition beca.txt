## CUBIST CONCENTRIC COMPOSITION In the course of time symmetrical composition became more and more centred around the vertical axis of the plane, and so much so that the composition was arranged in an entirely axial way leaving the borders of the canvas white and thus void. Christ, Mary and the Cross were replaced by Guitar, Bottle and Newspaper. II NEO-PLASTIC， ECCENTRICCOMPOSITION A very important, genuine renovation of compositional methods. Gradual elimination of the centre and any passive voids. Composition develops in the opposite direction. Composition gravitates towards the extreme boundaries of the canvas, instead of towards the centre, and indeed, implies continuation beyond these limits. This approach also implied the possibility of a compositional development in three dimensions.
