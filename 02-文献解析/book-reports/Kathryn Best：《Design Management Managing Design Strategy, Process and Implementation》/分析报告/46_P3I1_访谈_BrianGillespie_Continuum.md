# 46_P3I1：访谈——Brian Gillespie（Continuum数字设计主管）

## 一、章节定位与功能

### L2 定位
本访谈代表了"数字设计管理"视角——Brian Gillespie作为Continuum的Principal和数字战略与设计实践负责人，为全书注入了一个关键但其他章节相对讨论不足的维度：数字技术与设计的融合。

### L2 功能
- **数字整合功能**：阐述"digital connects"的核心主张——"digital is the glue that binds the customer experience"
- **全渠道体验功能**：从单渠道→多渠道→跨渠道→全渠道（omni-channel）的客户体验演进叙事
- **战略到实施的过渡功能**："strategy for strategy" vs."strategy for design" vs."strategy for design and implementation"——三种战略深度的区分

## 二至八节（概要）

### L2 核心论题
1. **"数字是黏合剂"**："Digital connects; it is the glue that binds the customer experience, it provides the pipes and tubes that flow the data and information that forms the lifeblood of contemporary service ecosystems."
2. **全渠道（omni-channel）**："Omni-channel is the complete and seamless integration within and across channels that delivers what appears to the customer as a seamless, integrated, consistent branded customer experience."
3. **服务蓝图（Service Blueprint）**："Our blueprints are becoming central not just to integrating the experience but also to integrating the teams we put together to deliver the experience."
4. **三种战略深度**：(1)"strategy for strategy"→识别机会领域/前端模糊；(2)"strategy for design"→深入机会区域的交付导向型战略；(3)"strategy for design and implementation"→从Learn to Build→Build to Learn

### L2 关键论点
- L3 "My fundamental mantra is that digital connects; that it is the glue that binds the customer experience."
- L3 "The flow of data and information between channels and devices and across contexts partnered with excellent human interactions that delivers the best contemporary experiences."
- L3 "We talked a lot about design thinking and design doing, but not enough about the implications on design managing."
