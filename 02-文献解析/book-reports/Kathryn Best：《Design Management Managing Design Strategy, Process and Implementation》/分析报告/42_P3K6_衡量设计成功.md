# 42_P3K6：衡量设计成功（Measuring the Success of Design）

## 一、章节定位与功能

### L2 定位
本章是Part Three中最具"商业性"的章节，直面设计管理中最棘手的问题之一——如何衡量设计的价值。核心挑战在于：设计在"硬"财务指标（利润/销售额/市场份额）和"软"体验指标（品牌形象/客户满意度/组织学习）之间的测度不对称。

### L2 功能
- **度量框架功能**：区分"硬标准"（开发成本/资本预算/利润率/销量/年销售增长/市场份额增长）与"软标准"（组织学习/改进流程/更好的企业形象/更便捷的沟通）
- **知识产权保护功能**：IP注册（商标/版权/专利/设计权/许可协议）作为赋予设计可度量价值的法律机制
- **非财务度量功能**：引入Triple Bottom Line、Equator Principles、设计奖项、同行评审等替代性度量方式

## 三至八节（概要）

### L2 核心论题
1. **CEO的三问**："how many sales have been generated? How much profit has been made? And what shareholder value has been created?"——设计经理必须能回答
2. **"80%的钱花掉了，只有20%的交付物可见"**："Most of the tangible results will appear towards the end of the project. It might worry the person managing the project budget, when 80% of the money has been spent and only 20% of the deliverables are visible."
3. **IP作为设计价值的法律载体**：Creative Barcode、ACID（Anti Copying In Design）——注册和保护的创意输出可以"generate additional revenue streams"
4. **三重底线（Triple Bottom Line）**："profit, planet, people"——将度量从财务扩展到环境可持续性和社会责任
5. **NHS的病人安全模型**："benefits are measured on the improvements made to the patient care experience – and the number of lives saved"——生命本身作为设计的度量

### L2 关键论点
- L3 "The hard truth is that most CEOs want to know three things about any business expenditure, including design: how many sales have been generated? How much profit has been made? And what shareholder value has been created?"
- L3 "'Measuring success in innovation by looking at the size of the R&D budget is like figuring out how successful a song (or a film or a book) will be by measuring how long the creator took to write it.' Vivek Kochikar."
- L3 "Whether a project is judged to be successful or not will depend on the business objectives, project aims and end goals defined in the brief."
