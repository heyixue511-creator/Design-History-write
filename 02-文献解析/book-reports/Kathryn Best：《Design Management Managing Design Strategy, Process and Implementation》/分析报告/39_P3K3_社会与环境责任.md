# 39_P3K3：社会与环境责任（Social and Environmental Responsibilities）

## 一、章节定位与功能

### L2 定位
本章是Part Three最具伦理分量的章节，将设计管理的讨论从"效率与利润"扩展到"责任与影响"。它回应了时间线中Victor Papanek (1972)对设计行业的伦理指控——"The designer's responsibility is to society and the environment, rather than to the client's bottom line"——并将其转化为21世纪的操作性设计管理实践。

### L2 功能
- **伦理框架导入功能**：将环境和社会责任系统化地纳入设计管理的全生命周期（原材料→生产→使用→处置）
- **企业社会责任(CSR)与设计的连接功能**：将CSR议程从公共关系的表层延伸到设计决策的核心
- **材料-流程-成本-处置四维分析功能**：提供一个系统的可持续设计决策框架

## 二至八节（概要）

### L2 核心论题
1. **全生命周期责任**："Design decisions will be made consciously and unconsciously and responsibly and irresponsibly, within the total lifecycle of a product or service."
2. **消费者愿意为"道德"付费**："Consumer trends indicate that people are willing to pay more for 'ethical' (healthier, safer, environmentally friendly or socially responsible) products and services."
3. **设计作为两种负责任设计的总称**："Environmentally aware design seeks to reduce the material and energy used and waste created." / "Socially aware design seeks to broaden the accessibility and inclusiveness of people."
4. **32Mb内存芯片的环境成本**：需要超过"a liter of fossil fuel, 32 liters of water and 72g of chemicals, which can include corrosive hydrogen fluoride"（Harris, 2006）
5. **Nike走向闭环生产**（closed-loop production）——无废弃物作为生产副产品

### L2 关键论点
- L3 "Design can, and frequently does, play a role in contributing to environmental and social problems, but taking a responsible approach to design brings the opportunity to shape a more environmentally and socially aware future."
- L3 "Companies that do not take a long-term view are missing important and rapidly shifting trends."
- L3 "Redesigning, and even rethinking the necessity of packaging, is a challenge that can be addressed through the design of, for example, refillable bottles or totally recyclable packaging."

## 八、实体清单
- **人物实体**：L3 **Victor Papanek**（《Design for the Real World》, 1972）；L3 **Anita Roddick**（The Body Shop创始人，隐含提及）；L3 **M. Harris**（关于内存芯片环境成本的数据来源）
- **组织实体**：L3 **The Body Shop**：基于品质和纯度采购原料/支持健康可持续工作条件；L3 **Nike**：走向闭环生产；L3 **Whirlpool**：重新设计洗衣机以减少能源和水消耗；L3 **Honda**：混合动力/更高燃油效率；L3 **Terra Plana**：99%回收材料鞋；L3 **Muji**：工业材料使用/可回收PET瓶/简约包装；L3 **ColaLife**：利用可口可乐供应链分发社会产品（Kit Yamoyo抗腹泻套件）；L3 **Genersys**：太阳能板技术
- **概念实体**：L3 **Total Lifecycle Responsibility**（全生命周期责任）；L3 **Closed-loop Production**（闭环生产）；L3 **Zero Waste Society**（零废弃物社会）；L3 **Ethical Consumerism**（道德消费主义）；L3 **Socially Aware vs. Environmentally Aware Design**（社会意识vs.环境意识设计）；L3 **Fairtrade / Soil Association / Timber stamp marks**（公平贸易/土壤协会/木材认证）
- **框架/模型实体**：L3 **设计全生命周期四阶段模型**：原材料采购→生产过程→使用成本→末端处置；L3 **Triple Bottom Line（隐含关联）**
- **术语实体**：L3 **Kitemarks**（质量认证标志）；L3 **Carbon footprint**（碳足迹）；L3 **Recyclable/Refillable packaging**（可回收/可再填充包装）；L3 **Hybrid electric motors**（混合动力电动机）；L3 **Supply chain**（供应链）
- **产品/项目实体**：L3 **Terra Plana Worn Again鞋**：99%回收材料（咖啡袋/T恤/牛仔裤/军用夹克）；L3 **Muji可回收/可再填充PET瓶**；L3 **ColaLife Kit Yamoyo**：10个套件恰好放入一箱可口可乐瓶颈之间的空隙；L3 **Whirlpool节能洗衣机**
