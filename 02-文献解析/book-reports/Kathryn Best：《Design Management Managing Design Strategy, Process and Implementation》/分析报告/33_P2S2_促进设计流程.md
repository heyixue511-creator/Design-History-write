# 33_P2S2：关键技能——促进设计流程（Facilitating the Design Process）

## 一、章节定位与功能
本章聚焦于设计经理作为"流程促进者"的角色——在保持设计团队创造力的同时确保流程按计划推进。核心工具是设计评审（Design Reviews）——来自Hollins & Hollins (1991)的"设计圈"（Design Circles）概念。

## 二至七节（概要）

### L2 核心论题
1. **设计评审作为跨职能对话平台**：设计圈——将组织内不同部门的小群体聚集起来评审设计——解决"people quite often do not understand what information the other needs to do their job"（Hollins & Hollins, 1991）
2. **Apple的并行设计评审模式**："Products are developed by all departments simultaneously – design, hardware, software – and in endless rounds of interdisciplinary design reviews"（Grossman, 2005）
3. **SMART目标设定**：Specific（具体）→Measurable（可衡量）→Achievable（可实现）→Realistic（现实）→Timed（有时限）
4. **设计经理的双向"翻译"**："Translating business terms to designers, and explaining design terms to clients, promotes clear communication and understanding on both sides."

### L2 关键论点
- L3 "It is a matter of experience and good judgement as to whether a design manager needs to bring the process back on track, or question the design process in relation to the brief without actually stifling the creativity of the design team."
- L3 "Design managers need to seek ways to safeguard the creative process, and to shield the design team from the unproductive comments of others."

## 八、实体清单
- **人物实体**：L3 **Gill Hollins & Bill Hollins**：《Total Design》作者，设计圈概念；L3 **Lev Grossman**：Time Magazine，Apple设计流程的报道者
- **组织实体**：L3 **Apple**：无限轮次跨学科设计评审的标杆
- **概念实体**：L3 **Design Circles**（设计圈）；L3 **Design Review**（设计评审）；L3 **SMART Objectives**（SMART目标）；L3 **Deep Collaboration / Cross-pollination**（深层协作/交叉授粉）
- **框架/模型实体**：L3 **Hollins & Hollins设计圈模型**：主圈+辅助圈的沟通结构；L3 **SMART目标框架**：具体/可衡量/可实现/现实/有时限；L3 **发散vs.收敛空间模型**：开放空间（刺激）vs.安静空间（专注）
- **术语实体**：L3 **Stage gates**（阶段关口）；L3 **Design circles**（设计圈）；L3 **Cross-functional teams**（跨职能团队）
- **项目实体**：L3 **Impact Hub**：全球"影响力创造者"社区空间
